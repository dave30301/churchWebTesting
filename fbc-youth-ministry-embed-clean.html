About Youth Ministry

 
Youth Discipleship Hour (YDH) meets during morning worship on the 1st and 3rd Sundays for grades K–12. Youth Ministry Groups meet the 2nd Saturday of each month at 11:30 AM. Teens in grades 7–12 can also join a Bible class on Thursdays at 7:00 PM on Zoom.
